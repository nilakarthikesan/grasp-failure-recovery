## What the argument establishes, and its limits

These are **conditional engineering arithmetic certificates**, not formally outward-rounded floating-point proofs. They inherit the recorded native distance/cell-radius assumptions, declared distance/transform/shell allowances, exact source/object frames and anatomical part assignment. The measured target distance concerns the union of individual palm collider surfaces, including buried interfaces; it is not an exterior physical-contact boundary proof. MANO's skin-weight part labels and Shadow body labels are an explicit correspondence, not established universal anatomical equivalence.

The strongest result rules out the frozen strict palm-preservation claim for this saved case at the current native-wrist-to-Shadow-palm POSITION, even under arbitrary palm rotation. It does not rule out another anchor translation, changed palm geometry, another part correspondence, a different mapping or another embodiment. It does not establish anything about all HUG proposals, physical lift success, native failure-label preservation, unseen-object generalization or predictor adequacy.

The earlier unsuccessful fits alone were not an impossibility proof. The new analytic geometry argument is the independent reason that simply increasing finger-fit iterations, wrist limits or GPU speed cannot make this fixed-anchor case pass.
