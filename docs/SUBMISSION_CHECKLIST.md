# Workshop submission checklist

Recorded venue: [Sim2Real2Field, CoRL 2026](https://sim2real2field-corl26.github.io/).
The selected Late-Breaking track permits four main-text pages, excluding
references, in the standard CoRL 2026 LaTeX template.

Recorded deadline: 15 October 2026, 11:59 PM Central Time (CDT, UTC-5).
Check the venue's current call before submitting.

- Review the title, research question, experiment status and claims with both
  authors. Completed diagnostics and proposed training comparisons must remain
  distinguishable.
- Format the existing working paper in the official template and check the
  main-text page limit and venue anonymity requirements.
- Verify references, result counts, figures, protocol versions and limitations.
- Confirm every author has an active OpenReview profile and collect profile IDs.
- Identify at least one author willing to review up to two workshop papers.
- Upload and verify the final submission and author list before the deadline.

OpenReview recommends a university, company or organization email for signup.
A coauthor employed by Google can use their Google work email for verification.
If a profile already exists, add and confirm that email on the same profile.
[Signup instructions](https://docs.openreview.net/getting-started/creating-an-openreview-profile/signing-up-for-openreview)
and [activation help](https://docs.openreview.net/getting-started/creating-an-openreview-profile/expediting-profile-activation).

This checklist records preparation tasks; it does not indicate a completed
submission or acceptance.
