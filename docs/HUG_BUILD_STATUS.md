## Workshop requirements checked against official sources

The [official workshop site](https://sim2real2field-corl26.github.io/) describes
the Late-Breaking Track as four pages excluding references, using the CoRL 2026
template, double-blind and non-archival. One author must review at most two
workshop papers. The public deadline is 15 October 2026 at 11:59 p.m. CDT:
**16 October at 12:59 a.m. America/New_York**. The public OpenReview Submission
invitation API gives 05:59 UTC, one hour later; use the earlier website deadline.
The [OpenReview venue](https://openreview.net/group?id=robot-learning.org/CoRL/2026/Workshop/Sim2Real2Field)
is the submission destination. Acceptance is an organizer/reviewer decision.

The form asks title, authors, keywords, abstract, track and PDF (at most 50 MB),
with optional TLDR/published venue. Authors must handle email-sharing/public
release consents, the CC BY 4.0 license and reviewer commitment. No form action
or consent was taken. The source has a limitations section and AI assistance
disclosure; metadata is in `docs/paper/submission-metadata.txt`.

contents, including previously uncommitted research sections, are retained at
