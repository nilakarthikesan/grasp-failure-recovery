# HUG mapping criteria provenance

15 October 2026. Read-only audit of local code, documentation, frozen protocols and six historical commits. No inference, geometry queries, fitting, physics or evaluation data were created. External-paper claims are reported here only as local attributions; the separate upstream-paper review must verify them.

**The checks are not a single HUG-paper validity standard.** They combine implementation correctness, project engineering safeguards and a deliberately strict geometric preservation claim. The latest nine strict failures remain failures under that claim. They do not establish that every useful approximate Shadow retargeter is impossible, nor that HUG failed its own benchmark.

## Geometry and evidence limitations

The [declared geometry contract](/Users/nilakarthikesan/.codex/worktrees/partner-simulation-review/grasp-failure-prediction/docs/HUG_MAPPING_ACCEPTANCE.md:85) compares distances to native open anatomical patches and literal individual collider surfaces. The [implementation](/Users/nilakarthikesan/.codex/worktrees/partner-simulation-review/grasp-failure-prediction/research_artifacts/hug_surface_constraints_audit_2026-10-15_v1/validate_candidate.py:34) takes unsigned per-collider distances; the union of collider surfaces is not the boundary of their occupied union. Interior interfaces can therefore matter to this literal metric. The [orientation implementation](/Users/nilakarthikesan/.codex/worktrees/partner-simulation-review/grasp-failure-prediction/research_artifacts/hug_orientation_uncertainty_acceptance_2026-10-14/orientation/check_orientation.py:80) separately excludes buried closest points and unreliable features. Neither result is a direct measurement of accessible force-bearing contact, and overlapping anatomical masks must not be summed into physical contact area.

The [normal-sign definition](/Users/nilakarthikesan/.codex/worktrees/partner-simulation-review/grasp-failure-prediction/docs/HUG_MAPPING_ACCEPTANCE.md:177) is geometrically interpretable, but matching signs only establishes agreement at eligible sites. Distance bounds do not bound discontinuous nearest-feature normals. Missing coverage stays unresolved. Making a previously failing relationship ambiguous is not evidence that it was repaired.

The [cell interval code](/Users/nilakarthikesan/.codex/worktrees/partner-simulation-review/grasp-failure-prediction/research_artifacts/hug_orientation_uncertainty_acceptance_2026-10-14/sampling/cell_intervals.py:1) uses the 1-Lipschitz property of distance to a fixed set and triangle radii, conditional on the stated arithmetic allowance. The [already completed identity control](/Users/nilakarthikesan/.codex/worktrees/partner-simulation-review/grasp-failure-prediction/research_artifacts/hug_surface_constraints_review_2026-10-15_v1/identity_interval_control.py:19) shows that independent intervals can retain positive possible mismatch for identical fields that straddle a band. This is a certificate limitation, not actual geometry mismatch or invalidation of definite counterexamples. The [latest completed study](/Users/nilakarthikesan/.codex/worktrees/partner-simulation-review/grasp-failure-prediction/docs/RESEARCH_DECISIONS.md:2464) still records nine strict failures after all thirty original tip caps and six mean guards passed.

## Better-grounded next assessment

1. Name the intended claim first: predicting the outcome of an executed, declared Shadow retargeting pipeline differs from predicting what the native MANO proposal would have done. Source-to-robot distortion remains a potential confound for comparing generator diversity.
2. Retain source/FK/frame/physical-limit checks. Choose and document the reference geometry and anatomical correspondence explicitly, including rendered surface, individual colliders, exposed union boundary and simulator contact conventions. Do not silently replace the frozen domain.
3. Keep the current bands, continuous per-finger distances, normal evidence and penetration/miss descriptors as measurements. Do not choose a new margin just above current failures or borrow a paper's aggregate error as a pass limit.
4. If the goal remains universal equality, develop a separately versioned paired/feature-stability certificate to reduce conservative uncertainty. This cannot erase existing definite mismatches. If the goal is approximate Shadow adequacy, specify a new prospective, claim-specific protocol using externally justified error allowances or separate calibration evidence; no such value is established here.
5. For downstream adequacy, later authorized outcome-sensitivity tests under a fixed controller and held-out objects should quantify whether mapping distortions materially alter results. The repeatedly inspected cases are development evidence, not an independent test set. Preserve poor native proposals, refusals and mapper versions; successful lifting alone is not a mapping gate.

All of the above are recommendations, not a new operational acceptance policy. No thresholds, historical results, code or shared documentation were changed by this audit.
