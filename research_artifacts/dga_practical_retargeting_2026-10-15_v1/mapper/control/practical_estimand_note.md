The prediction target is the outcome of the mapped Shadow attempt under a fixed execution protocol. Generator and retargeter versions jointly define the proposal pipeline. Static admission verifies that the mapped state is specified and realized correctly; it does not assert that its native hand would have the same outcome. Future comparisons therefore compare generator-plus-mapper pipelines, and must retain mapping distortion and refusal accounting so a mapper-induced failure is not silently attributed to the native generator. No outcome information is used to choose corrections or admit poses. Source-invalid/unrepresentable proposals stay in coverage records and are not relabeled as simulated failure episodes.
