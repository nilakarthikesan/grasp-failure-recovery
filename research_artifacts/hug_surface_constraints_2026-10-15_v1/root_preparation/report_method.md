# HUG direct surface-constraint study

15 October 2026. Static development study of six already saved native HUG grasps: apple A/B, milk box A/B and mug A/B (inventory indices 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28). This study retains Shadow and the frozen strict native-relative scientific criteria. It generates no new native proposals, physics episodes, evaluation labels or predictor training.

## Question

The previous per-grasp palm-placement studies found tip-feasible poses but did not preserve the native surface relationships. This study tests explicit constraints on saved palm/finger counterexamples instead of trading those failures against an average surface-fitting objective. The eventual predictor target remains the executed retargeted Shadow grasp; that choice does not certify equivalence to native MANO or loosen this study's criteria.

## Fixed evidence and constraints

The bound witness extraction retains 178 original whole-cell proximity examples (98 lost-native, 80 added-mapped) and 134 saved orientation records from the original and prior translated candidates. These records are capped examples, with duplicates retained across bands and studies, not all failing cells. Original caches contain 44,558 definite mismatch tuples across nine grids, six anatomical parts and three bands; 44,380 are outside the retained 178 examples. These counts overlap and are not independent sites, physical contacts or unique area. Mug A has no retained reliable orientation witness; that absence is not an orientation pass.

For each lost-near example, target distance plus the original cell radius and arithmetic allowance must stay within the source band. For each added-near example, target distance minus those bounds must be strictly beyond the source band. Moving a mismatch into an uncertain interval is insufficient. Normal constraints preserve the source sign, including native-away orientations. Exact checks retain native anatomical labels, source bands, exterior status and the original normal/feature eligibility rules; invalidating a witness is not repair.

The state contains 24 named joint coordinates and three world-frame palm translations. Native world targets, actual Shadow geometry, physical joint limits intersected with the original ±0.35 rad correction domain, floating-root quaternion, five historical individual fingertip caps and the 10 mm mean guard are fixed. The mapped Shadow palm moves through the established root compensation, with static forward kinematics only.

## Certificate limitation

An independent manufactured identity control uses the literal frozen interval functions. For a positive-area native cell whose distance interval straddles a band, the separate interval formulas cannot make both lost and added upper bounds zero for any target classification, even when native and target distances are identical. This demonstrates uncertainty in that certificate, not a definite mismatch or physical impossibility. Zero known counterexamples would still require stronger paired/adaptive coverage evidence and continuous orientation coverage under the same scientific criterion. This study leaves the frozen evaluator unchanged.

## Interpretation limits

Every fit and full-grid check uses the same six already inspected development grasps. Finer repeats are repeated measurements, not new independent grasps. Finite optimization is neither a convergence guarantee nor proof that no feasible mapping exists. Literal part-collider surfaces can include buried interfaces; geometry agreement alone cannot establish dynamic contact, task-outcome preservation, real-world transfer or unseen-object generalization.

## Frozen numerical search

Protocol SHA-256: `898a9fe5353329690a775b9a018e8f0984b28f3926a0df9b35e5d6c5f950667b`. Two fixed starts per case use the original pose and a previously saved deliberate palm-translation candidate. SLSQP minimizes an epigraph bound on the worst normalized surface violation, with distance terms scaled by 1 mm and signed-normal terms dimensionless. Original fingertip/mean constraints remain outside that slack. Raw nearest-part and exterior margins guide the search; complete feature, burial and visual-object eligibility are exact checkpoint gates rather than differentiated constraints.

Each start has at most 40 optimizer iterations. Per case, at most 3,000 raw geometry callbacks and 90 exact checkpoints are allowed, with two reserved terminal checks; no extra starts or budget extensions are permitted. The raw count includes work inside exact checks but not cache hits. Only exact-checked, original-tip/domain-feasible starts, major iterates and terminals can enter diagnostic selection. All-witness-feasible states rank first; otherwise the fixed ranking uses invalid orientation rows, worst exact normalized violation, failed-row count and normalized pose displacement, with stable earliest ties. Repeated source rows are preserved; the maximization does not sum their residuals, but row-count tie-breakers count their multiplicity.

The previous per-part direction-SSE selector is measured separately. It is an auxiliary historical surrogate, not the frozen scientific acceptance rule, and does not replace direct constraints or the independent full-grid audit.

## First bounded run (V1)

V1 performs 6,585 raw surface callback executions and 66 exact checkpoints. No selected pose satisfies every retained witness. Apple A/B, milk box B and mug B retain their earlier deliberate-palm diagnostic poses; milk box A retains the original pose. Mug A selects a new 22.359525 mm palm displacement and satisfies 10 of its 57 proximity examples, while all original fingertip caps hold. The other selected proximity counts are 0/33, 0/15, 0/37, 2/14 and 0/22. Retained orientation counts are separately reported and must not be interpreted as rates because rows are capped, duplicated and pose eligibility can change.

There are 196 rejected major/terminal checkpoints, all violating an individual original fingertip cap, with no mean-tip or domain rejection. Apple A's two 40-iteration terminal states exceed a cap by approximately 0.582 and 0.108 mm; Apple B's first terminal exceeds a cap by approximately 0.00112 mm. These are unsuccessful search states, not evidence that the original fingertip constraints are inherently infeasible. The selected diagnostic poses all meet their exact caps.

## One separately recorded guidance correction (V2)

Saved terminal evidence exposes a flat search residual for far cells: the literal lower interval bound is `max(0, distance - error)`. Below zero, its derivative with respect to distance vanishes, even though the native-far requirement remains unsatisfied. For every positive source band, `max(0, y) > band` is equivalent to `y > band`. V2 therefore uses the unclamped expression for the optimizer's far-distance guidance only. Final literal predicates, source data, exact diagnostic ranking, two original/prior starts and all budgets remain unchanged. Independent manufactured controls verify the equivalence and unchanged final fields. A plateau can hinder the search; it is not established as the explanation for every unsuccessful trajectory.

Before V2 outputs and before any new full-grid measurements, the final diagnostic selection rule is fixed: compare the independently reviewed V1 and V2 selected candidate for each original grasp using the same exact diagnostic rank; exact ties select V1. No full-grid outcome or additional raw search state enters this selection. The full nine-comparison audit applies to those six selected poses. The two fitting versions remain distinct development evidence.

They satisfy the mean and domain gates but are refused before exact orientation evaluation. These small violations are not rounded into passes. ## Runtime deviation

The fitting protocols declared two Numba threads per worker. A nested imported geometry module reset the effective thread count to four; fresh import-only reproduction independently confirms this behavior for both versions. The two fit workers therefore used four threads each. This resource-control deviation is preserved in each primary bundle and the independent review; the frozen protocol is not rewritten. Scientific sources, starts, callback/iteration/checkpoint budgets, final predicates and candidate selection were separately verified. The final audit explicitly resets and asserts two threads after imports. Numerical review does not certify compliance with the declared fitting thread cap.

## Next method to investigate

The corrected searches reduce some surface residuals but lose exact fingertip feasibility. A candidate next method is a bounded trust-region search with explicit restoration of each proposed joint-and-palm step into the five original fingertip caps before admitting surface progress. This is proposed, not implemented or proven feasible. It must preserve native-away relationships and reject steps that merely make witnesses ambiguous or ineligible. A successful retained-example fit would still require complete mapping checks and stronger coverage evidence; it would not by itself authorize evaluation data generation.
