\subsection{HUG}
HUG supplies MANO hand hypotheses from observations \cite{hug}. Our conversion
places the native wrist at the Shadow palm position and retargets object-relative
landmarks through Dex Retargeting \cite{dex}. Native and target hands have
different surface geometry, so numerical reconstruction and correct forward
kinematics are necessary but insufficient evidence of grasp preservation.

All 32 saved native exports reconstruct exactly in the recorded 3D audit;
this does not assert all-32 camera-projection equivalence. Detailed surface
measurements on six existing apple, milk-box and mug development cases, together
with three finer repeats, reject the declared strict preservation claim.
Definite lost/added proximity regions and reliable sampled orientation reversals
remain counterexamples. The nine resolutions are repeated measurements of six
grasps, not nine independent physical failures.

The first experimental local fit adds five individual fingertip nonworsening
constraints, a separate strict 10 mm mean-error guard and native-relative facing
targets, including away-facing targets. Final engineering assessment separately
declares a 10 nm arithmetic allowance; original tip errors are approximation
errors, rather than scientific acceptance margins. None of the six fits provides
an eligible improving candidate; each returns its exact original joint/root pose.
Independent source checks pass, while the unchanged surface audit returns zero
PASS and nine FAIL. Pointwise orientation separately has seven FAIL and two
UNRESOLVED results. This is not a repaired mapping or evidence of global
infeasibility. Physical contact and failure-label preservation remain unconfirmed.


A subsequent time-zero diagnosis reproduces all 649 recorded fingertip vectors
exactly and finds full-rank local fingertip-cap Jacobians in all six cases.
Nonzero inward steps satisfying the original caps exist. The unsuccessful
surface-optimization trajectories, rather than contradictory fingertip caps,
explain the first method's refusals; nearby direction-eligibility discontinuities
are observed but are not established as the cause of every refusal.

A separately frozen restoration stage projects each previously rejected raw
pose using cheap forward kinematics before evaluating the unchanged surface
objective. All six restored poses improve that objective by 1.52--38.09\% while
satisfying all 30 individual original-relative fingertip caps in the declared
computed arithmetic and the strict 10 mm mean guard. The projection targets
are tightened inward by 1 nm; no acceptance allowance is added. Fresh terminal
checks bypass the cache, and independent source-URDF versus captured-scene
checks pass. This stage uses 18 full surface evaluations in 49.57 s on the
existing development cases, with no new inference or physical episodes. These
are feasible improving points. Two cheap projections converge and four reach
their 40-iteration budget; final feasibility is checked independently of the
optimizer's status. This is not a full-convergence or surface-preservation claim.

The unchanged independent mapping audit gives zero PASS and nine FAIL; all
nine pointwise orientation results now FAIL. Definite lost/added proximity
regions remain despite the improved fitting cost. For example, the coarse
2 mm milk-box B summed added-area lower bound changes from 142.31 to
245.00 mm$^2$, while its fitting cost falls by 38.09\%. At the coarse 5 mm
band, mug A supplies seven reliable sampled sign-reversal witnesses, compared
with none observed previously. These are conditional anatomical-part area
sums and pose-specific eligible sample counts, not unique contact areas or
comparable failure rates. They show that the average fitting objective and
tip constraints do not certify the complete native grasp. The production
mapper therefore remains unchanged; continuous physical contact and
failure-label preservation are still unconfirmed.

A post-hoc saved-array diagnosis finds that the exact fixed-facing soft-L1
contribution worsens in all six lower-total-cost fits. Each invalidates
49--104 originally valid fixed direction bins. Of 43 unique saved coarse
orientation witnesses, 20 were selected direction-fitting sites, three were
originally eligible but unselected, and 20 were outside the original eligible
stratum. These capped examples are not all counterexamples or independent
grasps; fine-grid indices are treated separately. Sparse coverage alone
therefore does not explain the observed failures.

We implement a conservative optional development selector that refuses any
per-part fixed native-relative direction-SSE increase or original-valid site
invalidation, preserving all frozen targets and weights without renormalizing
missing mass. Thirty-six new manufactured controls pass, together with all
182 installed surface API checks. Separately frozen replay refuses all six
previously selected candidates and accepts six baseline identity controls;
all 30 saved fingertip caps still pass. Independent replay reproduces the
reports and per-part arithmetic exactly. Identity acceptance verifies only
the selector, not the original mapping. This replay runs no new geometry fit or physical episode; all nine strict
mapping results remain FAIL and production is unchanged.

A subsequent separately frozen guarded geometry study performs 134 full
surface callbacks and refuses all 122 completely scored nonbaseline
candidates. All six cases reach their FK budget and retain exact original
complete scene poses. Fresh independent source/FK checks and all 30 computed
tip caps pass. With bitwise-identical poses and unchanged measurement inputs, we explicitly
reuse the nine original surface reports, rather than repeat identical grids:
strict preservation remains zero PASS and nine FAIL, with seven FAIL and two
UNRESOLVED pointwise orientation results. Finite search refusal alone is not
an infeasibility proof.

Separately frozen geometric diagnostics identify a stronger limitation for
one saved mug grasp. Complete binary covers certify a lost native-near palm
relationship throughout the current wrist correction rectangle and, separately,
throughout the full physical wrist limits. Independent source/FK and analytic
box calculations reproduce all 149 local and 171 full-wrist leaf distances.
A radial-shell bound then removes the wrist-angle restriction: with the native
wrist/Shadow palm POSITION fixed, every rotated target palm surface lies within
97.349985 mm of that anchor. One previously recorded object cell of area
3.963678 mm$^2$ lies at least 103.996112 mm from the anchor, while its whole-cell
native distance is at most 4.745913 mm. After the declared numerical allowances,
its target distance is at least 6.645927 mm, exceeding the frozen 5 mm band
for any palm orientation. An independent 90-digit arithmetic/provenance review
passes 687 checks. These are conditional engineering arithmetic certificates
for literal individual collider surfaces, including buried interfaces, rather
than formal outward-rounded or physical-contact proofs.

This fixed-anchor counterexample cannot be repaired by additional finger
fitting, wrist-limit expansion or palm rotation. It does not rule out another
anchor translation, geometry or mapping, and supplies no native failure label
or generalization result. Source inspection finds the current wrist-to-palm
convention intentional, with no demonstrated missing origin transform. A new
anatomical calibration would require a separately declared mapping, consistent
placement/reference vectors and the unchanged independent acceptance audit;
no such repair is claimed here. The current version remains unvalidated for
strict native surface preservation. No new generator inference, physical
evaluation data or predictor training is produced by these static studies.

A subsequent source-only calibration constructs one proper rigid transform
from fixed neutral MANO MCP and rigid Shadow knuckle frames, independently
of object geometry or observed contact errors. Its translation norm is
8.668706 mm. All six new retargets pass numerical reconstruction, physical
joint limits and the 10 mm mean-tip gate, but 21 of 30 original individual
tip caps fail; zero candidates are eligible. Independent reconstruction of
the frame algebra, source/scene FK, original world targets and exact original
caps confirms these results. The primary neutral MANO descriptor is inherited,
not independently regenerated by this verifier. Runtime body-fusion and
joint-order compatibility stops are retained. The construction, targets and
criteria stay fixed, and V3 requires byte-identical calibration to V2.

After the calibration is fixed, a separate radial-shell diagnostic refutes
both saved mug anchors under arbitrary palm orientation. Three old cell rows
for mug A and two for mug B have positive whole-cell margins, with maximum
strict band excesses 7.135419 and 10.091852 mm after unchanged allowances.
An independent 90-digit replay passes 1,093 checks and retains nonpositive
rows. These overlapping-resolution witnesses are not independent failures
or additive physical contact areas. They exclude only those fixed-anchor
formulations, not arbitrary translations, different geometry or all proposals.
No full-grid audit or production promotion is performed for the refused
calibrated poses. A common-robot retarget and a native-MANO embodiment define
different failure-label targets; future placement/collision/dynamics validation
must declare the intended target. No new native HUG proposal, physics,
evaluation data or predictor training is produced.

We next retain the common Shadow embodiment and allow three world-frame palm
translation coordinates alongside the same 24 joint coordinates, with fixed
native world targets, original physical/correction bounds and floating-root
quaternion. The original individual tip caps and mean guard remain unchanged.
A bounded search uses 762 full objective callbacks; after its finer pattern
radii are not reached, one separately frozen fine study with 56 seeds per grasp uses 348.
Both methods retain all six original fallbacks under the additional per-part
direction selector. These 1,110 callbacks are static optimization computations,
not independent grasps or physical task attempts.

A separately frozen diagnostic selection verifies objective comparability and
chooses one lowest-cost tip-feasible nonzero-translation candidate per case
without changing the guarded returns. All six diagnostics pass independent
source/FK checks and all 30 literal original caps; all remain refused by the
additional direction selector. Three selected shifts are only 1.165--3.284 nm and are
joint-dominated changes; milk-box B and both mugs shift by 3.716--4.892 mm.
The original approximation errors remain the caps, rather than independent
scientific accuracy margins. A lower average fitting cost is not a preservation
certificate.

Fresh full-grid measurements of those six diagnostic poses yield nine strict
and proximity FAIL results, with seven pointwise-orientation FAIL and two
UNRESOLVED results. No complete-pose surface report is reused. A provenance
review finds that the three nanometer shifts arose during constraint
restoration of initially zero-translation thumb-joint proposals. To address
this coverage gap, one separately frozen supplement selects only proposals
originating in the already declared translation seeds, using the same
objective and feasibility gates. Its actual shifts are 0.063565, 1.994976 and
0.031372 mm; all original tip caps pass, but all five fresh coarse/fine
comparisons FAIL both strict preservation and pointwise orientation. The
initial nine and supplemental five remain separate development measurements
of nine robot poses derived from six existing native grasps.

At the coarse 2 mm band, the two millimeter-shifted mug palms still have
certified lost-proximity lower bounds of 105.162 and 53.090 mm$^2$, unchanged
from their original bounds. Positive added-type thumb mismatch bounds are also
present; changing lower bounds alone does not prove a change in total error.
These conditional part-surface measurements do not establish physical contact
or failure-label equivalence. All guarded mapper outputs remain original,
production is unchanged, and no inference, physical evaluation data or training
is generated. A future method could constrain the actual counterexample cells
rather than trade them against average fitting cost, but this is unimplemented
and feasibility is unknown. Removing sampled failures would still require
continuous coverage evidence before complete sign-off.
