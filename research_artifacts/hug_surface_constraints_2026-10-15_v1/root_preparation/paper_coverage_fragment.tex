A separate manufactured identity control exposes a limit of the fixed-cell
certificate. For a native cell whose distance interval straddles a proximity
band, the inherited independent-interval formulas cannot make both lost and
added mismatch upper bounds zero, even when native and mapped distances are
identical. This is uncertainty in the certificate, not a definite geometric
mismatch or proof that retargeting is impossible. Removing the known
counterexamples would therefore remain insufficient for complete sign-off:
paired or adaptive coverage evidence, together with continuous orientation
coverage, would still be needed under the unchanged scientific criterion.
