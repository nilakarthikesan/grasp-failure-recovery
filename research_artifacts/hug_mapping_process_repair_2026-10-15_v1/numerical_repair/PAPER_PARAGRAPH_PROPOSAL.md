An implementation review identified two numerical reproducibility defects in the legacy HUG-to-Shadow vector retargeter. The sequential runtime retained the preceding proposal as its initialization, and its reported gradient contained a temporal regularization term absent from its scalar objective. A fixed saved-input A–B–A check showed an 11.02° maximum joint difference when A followed B; a central-difference check isolated the unmatched gradient term to within 1.08×10⁻¹¹. We implemented a separately identified static profile that resets before each independent proposal, removes the temporal term, records solver termination codes, and rejects failed or unavailable termination states. The repeated saved-input check became bitwise order-invariant. This repaired solver consistency rather than establishing geometric fidelity: source-URDF fingertip errors worsened for four of ten fingertips across the two tested proposals, so the profile was not promoted under the frozen nonworsening criterion. Historical mappings and the legacy default were retained, and no new model inference or evaluation episodes were generated.
