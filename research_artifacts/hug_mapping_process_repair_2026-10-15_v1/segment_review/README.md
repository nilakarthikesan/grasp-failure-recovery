# Independent finite segment review

The source review binds the fixed schedule and verifies original tip/domain checks, protected-row gates, strict lexicographic improvement and the fresh recheck. The initial draft had execution disabled; the final output review binds the authorized final protocol and all execution sources.

`final_output_review.json` and `review_segment_outputs.py` replay every saved candidate and exact check without importing a simulator or numerical optimizer. Both cases choose the first qualifying dyadic step, lambda = 1/16. Original individual fingertip caps and all previously passing/eligible pooled-reference records are preserved. The worst normalized violation decreases, but the number of failed retained records does not: 25 for Milk B and 23 for Mug B. The result is a diagnostic improvement on the fixed retained examples, not a full-grid mapping certificate or production promotion.

The executed study used 10 tip checks and 10 exact surface checks, including both fresh checks, and 44 static forwards including scene reconstruction. This reviewer performed no new geometry, FK, optimization, inference, simulation steps, labels or evaluation-data generation. Historical nine strict failures remain unchanged; no new full grids were run.
