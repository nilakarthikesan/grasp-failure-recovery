# Analytic contact dynamics — source only

This is a separately frozen numerical qualification of standard MuJoCo3.4.0 rigid contact. No actual model, forward, step, native geometry or dynamics experiment is executed by source creation or manufactured tests. Native MANO contact, articulated skin, real-hand calibration, task collection and prediction training remain unqualified even if all these controls pass. The new exact-skin Jacobian and penalty contact law is a different model: `contact_factory.py` provides only a future interface, and the current runner explicitly rejects that model ID and callback installation. A future law needs its own frozen analytic repeat; passing stock MuJoCo cannot qualify it.

The plan has eight fixtures and fourteen conditions: two static full3D Jacobian/virtual-work controls (one sphere/single-owner flex triangle and one sphere/solid plane, each in a nonidentity rigid frame); analytic freefall; sphere drop/settling/support on flex; matched solid-plane support; subthreshold soft-friction creep; superthreshold sliding; and isolated head-on sphere impact. Each of the six dynamic fixtures runs once at 1ms and once at0.5ms. Horizons are0.25,1,1,0.5,0.5,0.3seconds respectively. Exact caps are14 model attempts,14 data constructions,14 initial forwards,10650 step attempts,10652 contact-force reads,120seconds worker and130seconds outer timeout. Compilation may do internal engine work; counters describe explicit Python API attempts. No retries, candidate replacement or outcome-selected quota is allowed.

All masses, inertias and geometry are analytic synthetic inputs: sphere radius10mm, mass0.2kg (second impact sphere0.4kg), sphere inertia2mr²/5; a finite two-metre triangle patch, zero flex radius, or a solid plane. The two spheres initially approach at+0.2/-0.1m/s with zero net linear/angular momentum. The friction sphere has three orthogonal translation slides and no rotational DOF, so rolling cannot masquerade as sliding. Its motion remains inside the finite patch. No assets, includes, native poses, checkpoints or inference are consumed. The prior completed3.4.0 primitive protocol/report and independent readback are checked and bound separately, including their preserved older sources/runtime. Only the isolated --no-deps MuJoCo3.4.0 target is imported; base NumPy remains2.2.6.

The fixed solver is Euler, dense Jacobians, elliptic cones,100 Newton iterations, tolerance1e-12, impratio1 and no noslip postprocessing. Contacts have constant impedance d=0.9, direct solref(-10000,-200), zero margin/gap and tangential friction0.5 where condim3 is used. Head-on sphere impact uses condim1. These solref quantities parameterize constraint acceleration, not calibrated material N/m. For an independent scalar normal constraint, constant impedance gives a_c=d a_ref+(1-d)a_u, direct-mode k=stiffness/d. At rest a_c=0 and a_u=-g imply penetration δ=g(1-d)/stiffness=98.1µm. The same uncoupled scalar model gives interior-cone tangential creep v=(1-d)F/(m*damping): at F=0.25µmg the reference is0.000613125m/s. This explicitly is not exact static stick. At F=1.5µmg, saturated sliding acceleration is(F-µmg)/m=2.4525m/s². The model source, not outcomes, fixes these references.

`analytic_checks.LIMITS` is the complete predeclared gate list. Central limits are Jacobian1e-10, virtualwork1e-10W; discrete Euler freefall1e-10m/m/s; stationary penetration98.1µm±5µm, support within1%mg and settled speed≤1e-4m/s; soft creep within max(5%,1e-5m/s); sliding acceleration within5%; constant-mass impulse balance1e-8N and discrete midpoint impulse-work residual1e-9J; isolated-pair momentum1e-9kg·m/s, angular momentum1e-10kg·m²/s, measured action/reaction1e-9N and torque1e-10N·m. Impact mechanical energy may not exceed initial energy by more than1%. This energy screen excludes unrecorded real-material claims; all engine and independent energy traces are retained.

Primal/dual cone and complementarity residuals are independently evaluated from gradient g=Jqacc+Rf-a_ref. The primal force must satisfy Fn≥0 and ||Ft||≤µFn; its dual must satisfy gn≥µ||gt||; f·g must be near zero. Declared relative bounds are1e-8 for force feasibility and1e-7 for acceleration/complementarity, with explicit1+norm scales. These are solver numerical checks, not friction-parameter identification. Full normal and two tangent contact rows, raw R/f/a_ref, contact frame, ownership and point are saved. The static virtual-work test compares actual contact J against independently constructed rigid point-velocity Jacobians; body-force transmission is also compared. Momentum/action-reaction use actual generalized engine forces for the isolated pair; fixed-floor reactions are not represented as independently measured body dynamics.

Timestep comparisons are predeclared: freefall uses its exact semi-implicit Euler position-bias difference and1e-10m/m/s readback tolerance; settled support positions must agree within5µm; other final positions within0.1mm, except sliding's1% analytic motion budget; final velocities within1mm/s, except sliding's1% analytic speed budget. The matched flex/solid support comparison separately requires final positions within5µm and mean support forces within1%mg at each timestep. Both timestep runs must independently pass their analytic checks. Values are not adjusted after outcomes. Finite analytic failures remain FAIL in the exact complete schedule.
