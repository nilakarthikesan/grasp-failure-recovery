source-only canonical MCP frame and the six retained saved HUG development
proposals. It performs no fitting, calibration adjustment, simulator call,
object-to-hand geometry query, new sampling or physics integration.

archived old-anchor independent review; that historical code/result is preserved.

are multiplied independently, then their wrist translation plus wrist rotation
times the immutable canonical C translation gives the new palm origin. No C is
applied to scientific native WORLD targets. The C algebra and actual six-case FK
are separately reconstructed by the bound canonical independent verification.

All eight actual compiled palm box local poses/sizes are read from the frozen

nearest face clearance, an exterior point uses axis-overflow Euclidean norm.
Source URDF Decimal RPY/Taylor rotations independently reconstruct corresponding
corners in a separate 1.1e-7 m representation check. That check does not enlarge
any scientific shell/distance/transform allowance.

All seven inherited whole-cell witnesses are retained, including nonpositive
ones. Their coordinates, positive areas and original native centroid distances
are checked against exact saved query buffers. The frozen whole-cell radius is
used on both sides of the shell, and the same native-near/distance/transform

reconstruction from the earlier bound verification, which is hash-bound here.
Mug A cell radii and native geometry remain inherited from the frozen earlier
cell study; this replay does not independently regenerate those meshes/cells.

For every palm rotation, its surface points stay in the palm-origin radial
shell. A cell's distance from that origin lies between its centroid radius minus
and plus the cell radius. A strictly separated shell, together with the native
whole-cell upper proximity bound, therefore refutes the fixed new anchor for
every palm orientation. A nonpositive margin remains UNRESOLVED. This is a
conditional numerical certificate under the unchanged separately declared
allowances, not a formally directed-rounding proof.

All six original-cap refusals are cross-bound to the independently reconstructed
case results and preserved. This diagnostic cannot approve a refused pose or
waive a gate. Its scope excludes arbitrary translation, another calibration,
other hand geometry, unseen proposals, continuous full contact equivalence,
failure-label preservation or prediction-model adequacy. No full surface audit
